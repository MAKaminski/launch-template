## Why this exists

Most ideas die in setup. You spend the first two days wiring the same five services
together, lose the momentum, and never get to the part that actually tells you
whether anyone wants the thing.

This template collapses that to one command. Every launch shares one PostHog project,
one Supabase project and one Stripe account — joined by a `launch_slug` — so running
ten ideas in parallel costs about the same as running one.

## What's already wired

- **Analytics** that survives ad blockers, with a typed event taxonomy
- **A database** with row-level security on from the first migration
- **Payments** with idempotent webhooks that won't double-charge
- **Scheduling** embedded, and attributed back to the visitor who booked
